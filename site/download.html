Download Icarus

Free for Windows, macOS and Linux. Your diary stays on your computer, in a folder you choose.

  • Windows

    Your system

    Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit

    Download installer MSI package

    First launch

    The installer isn't signed yet, so Windows may say it protected your PC. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

  • macOS

    Your system

    macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon and Intel

    Download disk image

    First launch

    1. Open the disk image and drag Icarus to Applications.
    2. Open Icarus. macOS stops it because the app isn't notarized by Apple yet.
    3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose Open Anyway.

  • Linux

    Your system

    64-bit x86

    Download AppImage
    .deb .rpm

    First launch

    Make the AppImage executable from its file properties, or run chmod +x Icarus-Diary-*.AppImage. The .deb and .rpm packages install through your package manager.

Verify your download (SHA-256)

The builds aren't signed yet. Compare the checksum of the file you downloaded with this list.

Prefer not to install anything? Open the web app.
