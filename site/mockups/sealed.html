Only you can read this diary.

Icarus is a free, open-source diary app. Entries are encrypted with your password and kept on your own device. No account, no cloud, no one else.

Download Open the web app

For Windows, macOS, Linux and Android, or right in your browser.

One entry, two ways to look at it

Icarus encrypts with AES-256 and a key made from your password with Argon2id. Your device holds the file; only your password turns it back into words.

No reset link. No back door.

There is no account, so there is nothing to reset. When you set a password, Icarus gives you a recovery key. Keep it somewhere safe; it is the only other way in.

Questions

Is Icarus free?

Yes. Icarus is free and open source under the GNU GPL v3. There are no accounts, ads or subscriptions.

Where is my diary stored?

Only on your device. The desktop app saves it in a folder you choose, the Android app keeps it in its private storage, and the web app keeps it in your browser's storage. Icarus has no servers that receive or store diaries.

What happens if I forget my password?

Use the recovery key you saved when you set your password. Without the password or the recovery key, no one can open the diary.

Can I bring the diary I already have?

Yes. Import any .ics file and each entry appears on its day. You can export your diary as .ics at any time.
