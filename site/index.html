A private diary
for all your years

Icarus is a free, open-source diary app. Lock it with a password, keep every entry on your own device, and return to any day in an instant.

Download Open the web app

For Windows, macOS, Linux and Android, or right in your browser.

Everything a diary needs.
Nothing that gets in the way.

  1. Write the day down

    A calm editor with Markdown, spell check and autosave. Unfinished entries wait for you in Drafts.

  2. Come back to any day

    Pick a day on the calendar and read it as you wrote it. Days with several entries let you page through them.

  3. Find it, whatever the year

    Search titles, places and text across every entry as you type, and jump straight to the day.

  4. See how far you've come

    Entries, characters written and your fullest days, for the last month, the last year or all time.

  5. Lock it when you step away

    With a password, every entry and draft is encrypted, and the diary locks itself after a few idle minutes.

In your pocket too

The Android app is the same diary, made for one hand. Swipe between days, write on the bus, and lock it with the same password.

  • No account. Nothing to sign up for and no server that ever sees your diary.
  • Your own file. Entries live in a standard .ics calendar file. Import your old diary, export it whenever you like.
  • Free and open source. GNU GPL v3. Anyone can read the code and check how a diary is handled.

Questions

Is Icarus free?

Yes. Icarus is free and open source under the GNU GPL v3. There are no accounts, ads or subscriptions.

Where is my diary stored?

Only on your device. The desktop app saves it in a folder you choose, the Android app keeps it in its private storage, and the web app keeps it in your browser's storage. Icarus has no servers that receive or store diaries.

What happens if I forget my password?

Use the recovery key you saved when you set your password. Without the password or the recovery key, no one can open the diary.

Can I bring the diary I already have?

Yes. Import any .ics file and each entry appears on its day. You can export your diary as .ics at any time.
